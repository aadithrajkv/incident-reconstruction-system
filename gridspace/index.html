Location not working on a Mac?
  1. Keep Wi‑Fi turned on. Your Mac uses nearby Wi‑Fi information to determine location.
  2. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, allow your browser.
  3. Allow location for this website in your browser’s site settings, then retry.
  4. Try finding your location in Apple Maps. If that also fails, the issue likely extends beyond this website.

You can browse a saved or approximate area in read-only mode when available. For sharing, try opening Thotox on your phone and allowing location there.
